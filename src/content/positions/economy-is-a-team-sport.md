## The position

A society cannot function if everyone opts out. But a society is already malfunctioning if meaningful participation requires chronic burnout.

## Strongest counterargument

No system can guarantee that every participant experiences effort as worthwhile, and widespread withdrawal shifts costs onto those who remain.

## What would change my mind

Evidence that disengagement is primarily driven by preference rather than low perceived returns, insecurity or unsustainable demands.
