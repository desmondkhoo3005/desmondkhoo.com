# Content guide

## Decisions — what I chose

Use for a real choice with context and consequences.

Suggested backbone:

1. My judgment
2. Context
3. Options
4. Trade-offs
5. Evidence
6. What would change my mind
7. What happened next
8. Revision history

## Positions — what I believe

Use for an argument or opinion.

Suggested backbone:

1. The position
2. Why I believe it
3. Strongest counterargument
4. Where my argument may fail
5. What would change my mind
6. Revision history

## Guides — what I have learned

Use for explanatory and educational work.

Suggested backbone:

1. What it is
2. Why it matters
3. How it works
4. Practical example
5. Common misunderstandings
6. Risks and limits
7. Where to go next
8. Last reviewed

Standardise the thinking, not every visible heading.
